# E-commerce Customer Analytics
### Data-Driven Customer Insights & Business Growth Strategy

## Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end analytics solution for an e-commerce business using customer, transaction, marketing, and coupon datasets. The objective is to uncover actionable business insights that improve customer acquisition, retention, revenue generation, marketing effectiveness, and overall business performance.

The project combines exploratory data analysis, customer segmentation, statistical analysis, cohort analysis, and business intelligence techniques to support data-driven decision making.

---

# Business Problem

E-commerce companies generate vast amounts of transactional and customer data but often struggle to transform it into actionable insights.

This project answers critical business questions such as:

- Which months acquire the most customers?
- Which customers generate the highest lifetime value?
- Are marketing campaigns profitable?
- Do discount coupons improve revenue?
- Which products perform the best?
- Which customer segments deserve the highest marketing investment?
- How do taxes and delivery charges affect purchasing behavior?
